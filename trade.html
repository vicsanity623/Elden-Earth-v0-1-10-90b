Trading between players

Every trade in Elden Earth is friend-to-friend and protected by a sealed commit-reveal flow — nobody can swap a legendary for a common after you've committed.

How trading works

Trading exists to complete collections and build friendships — not to run a marketplace. There are no listing fees, no middlemen and no strangers: you trade with friends you've added in the game, one plot for one plot.

The sealed commit-reveal flow

  1. Open a trade from a friend's profile. Either side can start one.
  2. Both players select a plot — selections are sealed, so neither side can see what the other picked.
  3. Both commit. Once both plots are committed, the trade is locked in.
  4. The reveal. The plots swap atomically on the server — if either side walks away, nothing moves and both plots stay where they were.

Why sealed? The classic trade scam is "I'll show you mine if you show me yours" — then swapping to something worse. Sealed commit-reveal makes that impossible: the choice is final before anything is revealed.

Requirements

  • Both players must be friends in the game (friend requests live in the Friends tab)
  • Accounts must be at least 14 days old — this blocks throwaway accounts from being trade mules
  • Both sides need at least one tradeable plot (plots in your bag work too)
  • Trades expire automatically if the other player doesn't respond

These multipliers are community conventions, not game rules — the game never forces a trade. If a deal feels rushed or too good, walk away; the plot isn't going anywhere.

Tips for collection hunters

  • Trade duplicates, never progress. Keep one of everything before trading spares.
  • Lucky plots compound. A lucky plot earns +10% forever — over months that outvalues its trade premium.
  • Friendship hearts matter. Max friendship (10 hearts) improves lucky re-roll odds on gifts and trades.
  • Use the community hub. Post in the Trading tag to find partners outside your friend list.

Scam protection: the game will never ask for your password, and no staff member will ever ask for account access. Report anyone who does via the in-game report button or contact.

Daily gifts

Every friend pair can exchange one gift per day — small EB and a friendship XP boost. It's free, it's fast, and it's the cheapest way to level hearts toward those lucky trade bonuses.
