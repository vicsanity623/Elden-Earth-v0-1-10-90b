Getting started

Your first 30 minutes in the Realm — everything that matters, in the order that matters.

1. Sign in and meet your guide

Open the game and sign in with Google. The in-game guide walks you through the map, your treasury and the core buttons — take the 60 seconds, it pays off. New players also receive a New Explorer Bonus to spend on their first plots.

2. Claim your first plot

Tap BUY to drop into claim mode. The circle around your avatar is your purchase radius — every real 10×10 ft tile inside it can be claimed for 100 EB. Start with 4–6 plots around your home or workplace; that's your income engine.

Tip: plots you own earn Realm Coins 24/7, even while the game is closed. A cluster of 10 plots is a much better start than 10 scattered ones — cluster bonuses come from density in city rankings.

3. Collect diamonds

Look for floating diamonds on the map near you — tap to collect. Diamonds feed the Diamond Wheel, the Diamond Extractor and citadels. If you find an Elden Stop (a public landmark), spin it — stops drop EB and diamonds and everyone nearby benefits.

4. Spin the Diamond Wheel

The wheel lives in the Earn menu. You get 3 free spins every day, and each spin costs 2 diamonds after that. The wheel pays EB and diamonds — hit the 25 EB or 50 EB slices to bankroll your next plots. When free spins run out, a short ad can earn you more (optional — you can always just buy spins with diamonds).

5. Set up your income loop

  • Buy land whenever you pass 100 EB
  • Collect diamonds as you walk past them
  • Claim daily rewards — a 90-day ladder with big milestone days
  • Do the daily quests — the board pays EB and Reward Points
  • Open the game once a day to keep your streak alive

6. Add a few friends

Friends unlock daily gifts, trading and friendship hearts. After 14 days you can trade plots — rare for rare, or bundle commons for something better. The community hub is the place to find players near you.

First-week checklist

  • Own at least 5 plots
  • Claim the daily reward 3 days in a row
  • Complete 3 daily quests
  • Spin an Elden Stop
  • Activate your first 20X boost
  • Add 2 friends and send a daily gift
