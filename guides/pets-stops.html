Pets & Elden Stops

A companion that forages while you walk, and public landmarks that reward whole neighbourhoods. This is the social heart of the map.

Buddy, your companion

Every player has a pet — Buddy — who appears on your map and in your profile. Buddy isn't just decoration: feed him berries and he works for you.

  • Berries are gathered while you walk and play
  • Feeding Buddy sends him foraging — he returns with EB and diamonds
  • Feeding is a daily quest ("Feed a berry to Buddy" — +5 EB & +1 RP)
  • Buddy can be renamed and styled from the pet panel

Tip: feeding before a long walk is ideal — Buddy's foraging and your plot claiming stack into the same session.

Elden Stops

Real-world landmarks become Elden Stops — public hubs where players spin for rewards. Think of them as the game's meeting places: parks, monuments, popular corners.

  • Spinning a stop drops EB and diamonds, with a cooldown before the next spin
  • Everyone nearby benefits — stops are deliberately social
  • Beacon Seeds let players plant new stops at landmarks — the map grows where the community walks

Beacon Seeds

Beacon Seeds are rare items used to plant an Elden Stop at a qualifying landmark. Planting one is a permanent gift to your area — new players around it get a reason to keep walking. Beta-era players were granted starter seeds; more arrive through events and quests.

Play patterns that work

  • Feed Buddy daily — the quest alone repays it
  • Hit stops on your commute; stop cooldowns reset while you work
  • Plant Beacons at landmarks you visit often — your future self benefits
  • Stops + boosts + a walking route = the best free EB/hour in the game
