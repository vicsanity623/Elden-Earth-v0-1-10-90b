The Buddy Master Guide

Wild spawns, battle math, taming, genetics, training, breeding and the weekly PvP Arena — everything your fighting companion can do, in one place.

Your active buddy walks beside you on the map, fights wild buddies, defends your citadel, and battles in the weekly Arena. This guide covers the full journey — from your first wild encounter to a Level 50 legend.

🌍 Wild buddy spawns & tracking

Wild buddies appear as glowing orbs on your map as you walk.

  • You earn a spawn every 250 ft (76 m) of real walking
  • They appear 22–70 m away — a short walk to reach
  • Up to 3 wild buddies can be active at once
  • Each spawn lives for 8 minutes, then despawns
  • Daily cap: 50 spawns, resetting at midnight UTC

What counts as real walking: GPS accuracy within 60 m, genuine steps over 4 m, human pace (0.35–12 m/s — jogging and cycling are fine, cars are not), and new ground: you must be 25 m away from ground you already earned credit for 45+ seconds ago. Treadmills and walking in circles stop crediting quickly. Roughly 250 m of credit per minute maximum.

⚔️ Battles & XP

Tap a wild buddy orb to start a turn-based battle with your active buddy. Every outcome trains your buddy — here is the exact math:

OutcomeXP earned
Win(18 + opponent level × 7) × rarity multiplier
Feast (consume it)Full win XP + 75% bonus + 25, +12 happiness, full heal
Tame (befriend it)Full win XP + a new buddy for your bag
Let it go60% of win XP
Loss(18 + opponent level × 7) × 0.25 — losing still trains

Rarity multipliers: Common ×1 · Uncommon ×1.25 · Rare ×1.6 · Legendary ×2.4. Wild buddy levels track your strongest buddy, so fights stay competitive as you grow.

Watch the XP bar fill. At the end of every battle the results screen stays open and your buddy's XP bar fills with a glowing animation — "Buddy earned 87 XP during this battle!" — with a level-up celebration, sparkles and any newly learned moves. Tame and release battles show it too.

🤝 Taming & your Buddy Bag

  1. Win the battle — the wild buddy must faint
  2. Choose Tame — Befriend the Wild Buddy
  3. Feed it 30 berries across 3 feedings (~15 seconds)
  4. It joins your Buddy Bag 🎉
  • The bag holds up to 30 buddies — tap any buddy to inspect stats, IVs, nature and moves
  • Set one as your Active Buddy to battle with it
  • Rename buddies and change their hue / colorway — cosmetics never affect stats
  • Heal with berries: 30% HP per berry, and HP regenerates slowly over time

🧬 Stats, IVs, natures & rarity

  • Individual Values (IVs) — permanent genetics from 0–15 per stat: HP, ATK, DEF, SPD. Shown as a grade like Grade C · 63% IV — higher is better at every level
  • Natures — small stat personalities (Hardy and others) shown on the buddy card
  • Hue & colorway — pure cosmetics; mix and match freely
  • Rarity — Common · Uncommon · Rare · Legendary: better battle XP and base potential; the spawn orb's ring shows the tier
  • Level-ups recompute stats and fully heal the buddy

🎓 Training & leveling

Level cap: Lv. 50. Send reserve buddies to Training for passive XP over a timer (long sessions give the biggest drops), while your active buddy battles — two growth tracks at once. Short on time? Watch an ad for an Instant Training credit to claim early (3 ad credits per day). Your own trainer level grows from battles, tames, feasts and buddy level-ups.

💞 Breeding

Pick two parents from your bag — the baby is born fully grown at Level 5 with inherited IVs, stat mutations and a chance at a rarity upgrade.

  • Parents rest 7 days after breeding; each specific pair rests 30 days
  • Breeding costs EB + Diamonds
  • 🎬 Free Breed by watching an ad — up to 20 per day! One ad = one free breed: it waives the cost and the 7-day parent rest. The 30-day pair lock is never waivable. Credits bank up if you don't spend them

Strategy: breed your highest-IV buddies to chase S-grade offspring, keep one strong pair on rotation, and spend Free Breed credits on resting parents to skip the wait entirely.

⚔️ PvP Arena — seasons, rank & payouts

Weekly asynchronous PvP: your buddy battles other players' Defense Buddies — AI-controlled, exactly as strong as their owner set them.

  • Seasons run Monday → Sunday (UTC); the leaderboard shows the Top 500
  • Set a Defense Buddy — its snapshot is what opponents face
  • 1 AP per battle; free entries regenerate into a bank that fills to 5
  • 15 battles per day, resetting at midnight UTC
  • Sparring mode: practice vs a dummy — never moves rank
  • Win: +10 rank points · Loss: −5
  • Season-end payouts are staged by rank — RP, diamonds, Elden Stop Seeds and even plots — then claimed in-game
  • Every arena battle trains your buddy (full win XP, quarter XP on losses)

🍓 Berries, pets & ad boosts

Berries are the buddy currency — feed your pet (1 berry = +5 mood, high mood = +25% sprint speed and diamond scouting), heal buddies (30% HP each), and tame wild buddies (30 per tame). Earn them from Elden Stop spins (1–3 each!), badge rewards and dev gifts in your 📮 Mail box.

🏅 Badges & pro tips

Chase the buddy badges: Win 50 PvP battles · Tame 25 buddies · Feast 50 · Level a buddy 25× · Reach Lv. 50 · Own 15 buddies — paying diamonds, berries and RP.

  • Keep one tanky defender set in the Arena — defense wins farm rank
  • Feast weak spawns (bonus XP!), tame rare and legendary ones
  • Spin Elden Stops daily — berries fuel everything in this guide
  • Never battle with low berries — healing mid-hunt saves runs
