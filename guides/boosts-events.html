Boosts & events

Boosts are the multiplier layer of the economy — when to bank them, when to burn them, and how the big events change everything.

Boost basics

A boost multiplies your income — 20X normally. Each activation costs 2 EB and banks one hour. Bank up to 6 hours ahead of time; the bank can be extended to 10 hours with optional ads.

  • Activating repeatedly banks more time (the timer counts down while you play or rest)
  • Boosts multiply rent, wheel EB payouts and other income sources
  • Once banked, the boost runs automatically — no need to keep the app open

20X vs 50X

During 50X event windows, activations apply the super multiplier instead — the biggest income spikes in the game run on this calendar. 50X windows come with screen effects and community energy; they're the sessions you plan your week around.

Golden rule: a full 10-hour bank activated during a 50X window is worth five normal sessions. Bank cheap, spend big.

Global Challenges

Global Challenges are the community's shared goals — every player's EB earnings from the wheel and beyond feed a global pool that unlocks rewards for everyone when milestones fall. There's no entry fee: playing normally contributes.

  • Watch the challenge bar on the map screen
  • Spin and earn during the window to contribute
  • Claim your share of the pool when milestones unlock

  • Watch the in-game feed for event start times
