Citadels & sieges

Plant a citadel on your land, station your avatar as its defender, and mine diamonds while rivals look for a way in.

What is a citadel?

A citadel is a claimable stronghold you plant on one of your plots using a Citadel Capsule (awarded through play and events). Once planted and grown, it generates 2 diamonds/hour + 5 EB/hour for its defender — one of the best passive incomes in the game.

Defending

Station your avatar at the citadel to become its Reigning Defender. While you hold it:

  • Mining accrues automatically (2 💎 / hr, +5 EB / hr)
  • Spoils stop after 24 hours of holding — recall and re-station to keep earning
  • Daily spoils are capped (150 EB & 60 diamonds) so no one can stack an empire of unlimited income
  • Minimum hold before recall is 30 minutes; re-stationing has a 30-minute cooldown

Rhythm: station → hold ~24h → recall (bank the spoils) → 30m later re-station. Players who keep this cycle earn multiples over set-and-forget holders.

Recalling

Recalling banks your accrued spoils into your treasury and vacates the citadel. There's no reason to sit past the 24h spoils cap — recalls are how you collect. Optional: if you're inside the 30-minute minimum hold, a short ad lets you recall early.

Sieges

Think a citadel is yours by right? Prove it. Walk within 100 m of an enemy-held citadel, spend 1 diamond to initiate a Reflex Siege, and win the duel to dethrone the defender and claim a +5 EB bounty.

  • Sieges are location-based — you must physically be near the citadel
  • Defenders who are actively holding get their mining interrupted the moment they fall
  • Sieging is the fastest way to break a hoarder's rhythm — and the riskiest

Rarities & upgrades

Citadels come in rarities — common through legendary. Rarer citadels mine faster (legendary: 2 diamonds/hour, 5 EB/hour at the top end) and can be upgraded through the in-game upgrade flow. Rare citadels are also evolving holds — watch for growth timers.

Citadel strategy

  • Plant near your daily walking route so stationing is convenient
  • Keep the 24h recall cycle — idle citadels cap out fast
  • Watch the daily quests: recalling a defender is +15 EB & +2 RP; five recalls is +40 EB & +20 RP
  • Upgrading rarity multiplies everything the hold earns
  • Never station on a citadel you can't defend — sieges are real
