Economy & income

Three in-game currencies, one reward currency. Learn how they flow and you'll out-earn players twice your land count.

The three currencies

None of these have cash value. The redeemable currency is RP (Reward Points) — see the rewards page.

Passive rent math

Every plot accrues Realm Coins continuously at a rate set by its rarity and lucky status. The rate ticks in real time — your balance grows while the app is closed, and there's no cap on accrual. A legendary lucky plot is worth roughly double a plain legendary over a season.

Convert RC → EB anytime from your balance panel (RC 1 → 25 EB + 10 diamonds). This is the intended loop when you're saving for a land push: let RC accumulate, then cash it into plots.

The Diamond Extractor

The Extractor is an automated mining beacon at your home base. It accumulates diamonds over time (1 per 10 minutes at level 1) with a storage cap. Upgrades alternate between +1 capacity and -2.5% mining time — capacity first is usually right.

  • Collect before the storage fills — a full Extractor mines nothing
  • Upgrades scale in cost; they unlock as your cash balance grows
  • Watching an optional ad can double a harvest (free players just collect again later)

Boosts

Boosts multiply income — 20X normally, 50X during event windows. Each activation costs 2 EB and banks one hour, up to a 6-hour bank. Ads can extend the bank to 10 hours. Boosts multiply the whole economy: rent, wheel EB and more.

Smart spending order

  1. First 6 plots — income engine before anything cosmetic
  2. Extractor level 2–3 — passive diamonds compound fast
  3. Boosts during events — 50X windows are the best EB/hour in the game
  4. Ascension Forge — when plots are cheap no longer, upgrade instead of expand
  5. Trading — turn duplicates into tier jumps

Daily rhythm

  • Claim the daily login reward (90-day ladder)
  • Do the daily quests (up to 28 RP/day plus EB)
  • 3 free wheel spins
  • Collect the Extractor before it caps
  • Bank a boost before a long walk
