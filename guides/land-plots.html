Land & plots

The ground beneath you is the board. Here's how claiming, rarity, upgrades and the crowns of the Realm actually work.

Claiming land

Every plot is a real 10×10 ft square, priced at a flat 100 EB. You can only claim tiles inside your purchase radius — the circle around your real location — so the map grows as you move through the world. Plots can be claimed by anyone; if it's already owned, it's not for sale unless its owner relocates it.

Rarity tiers

Every plot rolls a rarity when created. Rarity drives its income rate and its trade value.

Lucky plots are special: a 0.5% chance when buying land, a small chance when re-rolling trades at high friendship, and they survive the Ascension Forge. That +10% compounds forever — long-term players treasure them.

The plot bag

Picked-up plots go to your bag instead of vanishing. From the bag you can place a plot anywhere legal inside your radius — this is how you rearrange your empire without re-buying anything. Bag contents are stored server-side, so they're safe across devices.

  • Pick up — removes a plot from the map into your bag
  • Relocate — place a bagged plot on a new empty tile
  • Ascend — upgrade a bagged or placed plot at the Ascension Forge

The Ascension Forge

The Forge upgrades plots one tier at a time: Common → Rare → Epic → Legendary. Each step costs resources and time. Ascension is the long-game sink for players who've outgrown simple expansion — and the daily quest board rewards it.

Mayor, Governor & President

Own the most land in a city to become its Mayor. The same contest runs at state level (Governor) and country level (President). Titles are recalculated as the map changes, and holders earn a 2% dividend on every plot bought in their territory.

Strategy: crowns are won on density, not sprawl. Ten tiles in one city beat ten tiles across three. Check the Realm Rankings before your next buying trip.

Collection strategy

  • Cluster purchases in one city to chase Mayor early
  • Keep one of every rarity before trading duplicates
  • Lucky plots are hold-forever assets — don't trade them casually
  • Use the bag to consolidate after a big walking day
  • Watch the daily quests — buying 5 plots in a day pays 90 EB back
