Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Elden Earth ("the Game") is a free-to-play mobile and web game. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

Information We Collect

  • Google Account Information: When you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account ID, display name, and email address for authentication and in-game identity.
  • Location Data: We access your device's GPS location to enable core gameplay features such as claiming land, territory exploration, and proximity-based actions. Location data is processed in real time and used to determine your in-game position. We also derive your country and, for US players, your state from that location — this is used to determine whether rewards redemption is available in your region.
  • Date of Birth: We ask for your date of birth once to confirm you are old enough to play (13+) and to redeem rewards (18+). It is recorded once and cannot be changed afterwards.
  • Phone Number: If you link a phone number, we use it to verify your account before any rewards redemption. This is handled by Firebase Authentication; we do not store your number ourselves.
  • Game Activity: We store your in-game progress including plots owned, currency balances, leaderboard rankings, referral relationships, and achievements. This data is necessary to save your game progress across devices.
  • Reward Points: Your Reward Points balance and the daily earning caps applied to it. Reward Points are a loyalty balance with no value outside the rewards program.
  • Redemption Records: If you redeem Reward Points, we store the PayPal address you provide, the amount, and the request status so that we can pay and audit it. PayPal is the payment processor and receives only what is needed to send your redemption.
  • Device Information: We collect device identifiers (such as Android ID) and hardware information for anti-cheat detection, preventing fraudulent accounts, and ensuring fair gameplay.
  • Chat Messages: If you use the in-game community chat, your messages are stored to enable the chat feature and enforce community guidelines.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the Game
  • To save and synchronize your game progress across devices
  • To determine territory ownership and leaderboard rankings
  • To detect and prevent cheating, exploitation, and fraud
  • To enable referral and social features between players
  • To display in-game activity feed items (e.g., "Player X claimed a plot")
  • To verify your age and your eligibility for the rewards program
  • To determine whether rewards redemption is legally available in your region
  • To process and audit rewards redemptions sent to your PayPal account
  • To serve and measure advertising (Google AdSense)

What Other Players Can See

Some game data is visible to other players as part of the world map and leaderboards — your display name, avatar, plots, titles, and in-game activity feed items.

The following are never visible to other players and are stored in a separate area that only your account can read:

  • Your Reward Points balance
  • Your date of birth
  • Your PayPal redemption details
  • Your phone verification status beyond a simple "linked" indicator

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Your data is processed using Firebase (Google), which provides our backend infrastructure.

We share data with third parties only in these cases:

  • PayPal: If you redeem Reward Points, we send PayPal the email address you provided and the redemption amount in order to pay you.
  • Advertising partners: Google AdSense serves advertising in the Game and may collect device identifiers under its own privacy policy.
  • Legal requirements: Where we are compelled to disclose data by law, or to protect the rights and safety of players.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase: Authentication, database, and cloud infrastructure
  • Google AdSense: In-app advertising
  • PayPal: Processing rewards redemptions
  • Mapbox / OpenStreetMap: Map rendering and territory visualization

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on Google Firebase servers with encryption in transit. We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information, but no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

Data Retention

We retain your game data as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Game. Redemption records — the PayPal address, amount, date and status of each redemption — are retained for accounting and fraud-prevention purposes, and for as long as required by tax and financial record-keeping rules. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us; note that we may retain redemption records where we are legally required to.

The Rewards Program

Reward Points are a loyalty balance earned through playing. They are not a currency, are not purchased, and have no value outside the rewards program. Points are earned through activity only and are never awarded for chance outcomes. Redemptions are subject to eligibility checks (age 18+, phone verification, regional availability) and to monthly and global program limits. See the Rewards Program Terms for the full rules.

Children's Privacy

The Game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the Game after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
