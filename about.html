About Elden Earth

A small studio building a big world — one real-world tile at a time.

The game

Elden Earth is a free-to-play geo-location strategy game where the real map is the board. Players walk their neighbourhoods, claim 10×10 ft plots of virtual land, and build a passive in-game economy from the ground they stand on. Land earns Realm Coins around the clock; the Diamond Wheel, citadels, pets, events and trading add layers of strategy on top.

It runs in any modern browser today, is in Google Play closed testing for Android, and is coming to iOS. One Google account carries your realm across every device.

Design principles

  • Free to play, fair to stay. No purchase is ever required — every system has a free path, and ads are always optional accelerators.
  • Skill and time over spending. Positioning, timing and good trades beat everything else in the realm.
  • Transparent economy. The rewards program has published rates and terms — see the Rewards page.
  • Built in public. The roadmap is public and the community shapes what ships next.

The studio

Elden Earth is developed by Elden Earth Studios, an independent team. The project started as a question — what if the ground beneath you was worth something? — and grew into a game about neighbourhoods, landmarks and the stories players build on top of them.
