One account, every device

Progress lives in your cloud save — not on your device. Signing in with the same Google account anywhere restores your land, currencies, badges and boost timers.

  • Sign in with Google — the same account works in the browser and the Android app
  • Plots, currencies, pets and badges sync automatically as you play
  • Switching phones or reinstalling never loses your realm
  • Public terminals and shared computers: just sign out when you're done

Playing on mobile data? Elden Earth is light on bandwidth — the map streams vector tiles and the game syncs only what changed. A typical session uses less than a social-media feed.
