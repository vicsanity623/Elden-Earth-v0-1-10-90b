Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the things players ask most. Can't find it here? The community hub or contact will sort you out.

Account & progress

Do I lose my progress if I switch devices?

No. Your realm lives in a cloud save tied to your Google account. Sign in on any device — browser or the Android app — and everything comes with you: land, currencies, badges, pets and boost timers.

Is my progress safe if I clear my browser data?

Yes — the cloud save is authoritative. Local data is only a cache. If a device ever shows an older state, the next sync corrects it.

Do I have to sign in with Google?

Signing in is required to save progress and to earn rewards. The Google sign-in is used only for your identity and email — never to read your messages or act on your behalf.

Gameplay

How big is a plot and what does it cost?

Each plot is a real 10×10 ft square of the world, and costs 100 EB. Your purchase radius is limited to the area around your real location — you claim land by being there.

What's the difference between EB, Realm Coins and diamonds?

EB buys land and boosts. Realm Coins (RC) accrue passively from the land you own. Diamonds power the wheel, extractor upgrades and citadels. None of them have real-world cash value. Reward Points (RP) are the separate, redeemable currency — see the rewards page.

Can I play indoors or while travelling?

Yes — but claiming new land requires your real location. Currencies keep accruing offline (boost timers, extractor output and rent all bank while you're away).

Trading & social

How do I trade with a friend?

Add each other as friends, then open a trade from their profile. Both sides select a plot, both commit — the swap is sealed until both are in. Full details on the trading page.

Why can't new accounts trade?

Accounts must be 14 days old. This keeps throwaway accounts out of the trade system and protects everyone's collections.

Rewards

Are the rewards real money?

Reward Points can be redeemed to PayPal once your account is verified and the Realm's community revenue goal is met. 100 RP = $1.00. Availability varies by region and age — details on the rewards page.

Do I need to buy anything to succeed?

No. Everything in the game is reachable through play. Ads are optional accelerators only — every ad placement has a free alternative beside it.

Technical

The game won't load — what do I do?

  • Check your connection and refresh the page
  • Enable location permissions when prompted (claiming land needs them)
  • Update your browser to the latest version
  • On iPhone, open the game in Safari rather than an in-app browser

How do I delete my account and data?

Use the in-game settings or the delete account page. Deletion removes your player data from our systems.
