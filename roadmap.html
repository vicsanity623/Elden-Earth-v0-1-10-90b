The road ahead

Elden Earth is built in public. This roadmap is our living plan — shipped items stay on it as a record, and dates move only when reality moves them.

Shipped

Live

The core realm

Real-world plot claiming, the passive RC economy, Mayor/Governor/President rankings with city dividends, the Diamond Wheel, boosts (20X/50X events), the Diamond Extractor, pets & berries, Elden Stops, daily login rewards and daily quests.

Live

Trading & social

Friend-to-friend plot trading with sealed commit-reveal, daily gifts, friendship XP and hearts, referrals, the community hub and this very portal.

Live

Progression depth

Ascension Forge (Common → Rare → Epic → Legendary), lucky plots, citadels with sieges & spoil caps, 63 badges across five tracks, and the Reward Points program.

Testing

Android closed testing

The native Android edition is in Google Play closed testing with local notifications (daily rewards, trade alerts, boost reminders) and a fully bundled game client.

In progress

Q3

Public Google Play launch

Opening the gates to everyone after the closed-test requirement is met. Includes store polish, onboarding improvements and server hardening for the launch wave.

Q3

World Events 2.0

Seasonal global challenges with shared milestones, bigger community pools and rotating reward themes — building on the current Global Challenge system.

Planned

Q4

iOS edition

Native iPhone & iPad build with the same cloud save. iOS players keep full access through Safari until then.

Q4

Guilds & territory wars

Form guilds, hold districts with your allies, and contest weekly territory control — the first step toward cooperative empire building.

Q4

More buildings, more choices

New plot buildings with tradeoffs — warehouses, watchtowers and shrines — so land layouts become a real strategy layer.

Later

Seasons & leaderboards

Monthly ranked seasons with titles, seasonal badges and end-of-season rewards for city, state and country champions.

Later

Live ops polish

More ways to customize your profile, richer in-game mail, and quality-of-life passes based on community feedback.

Want to shape the roadmap? Post ideas in the community hub — the feedback tag is read every week, and shipped features often start as player posts.
