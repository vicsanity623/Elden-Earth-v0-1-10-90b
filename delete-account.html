Account & Data Deletion

Elden Earth — last updated: October 16, 2026

You can request deletion of your Elden Earth account and the personal data associated with it at any time. Deletion is free and does not require any in-game action or purchase.

How to request deletion

Step 1. Contact us through either channel:

  • Discord (fastest): open the Elden Earth Discord and send a message to the moderators titled "Account deletion request".
  • Email: write to elden-earth-2026@googlegroups.com with the subject "Account deletion request".

Step 2. Include the email address of the Google account you sign in with, and your in-game name (if you can).

Step 3. We will confirm your request and complete the deletion within 30 days. Your account becomes inaccessible as soon as the request is verified.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in record and Google account link
  • Your profile: display name, avatar, and settings
  • All game progress: land plots, inventory, currency balances, Reward Points, achievements, and statistics
  • Chat messages and other content you posted in the game
  • Friends lists, trades, gifts, and other social connections
  • Device and session records tied to your account

What may be retained

  • Redemption records: if you redeemed Reward Points, the PayPal address, amount, date, and status of each redemption are retained for as long as required by tax and financial record-keeping rules.
  • Legal and fraud records: where we are legally required to retain limited records, or where they are needed to prevent fraud and abuse of the rewards program.

Once deletion is complete, your plots and territory return to the open world and your in-game name becomes available again. Reward Points have no monetary value outside the rewards program and are not paid out on deletion.

Questions

See our Privacy Policy and Rewards Program Terms, or reach us through the channels above.
