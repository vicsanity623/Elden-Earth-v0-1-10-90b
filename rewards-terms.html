Rewards Program Terms

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Please read this carefully. Elden Earth is a free-to-play game. Reward Points are a loyalty thank-you for playing — they are not earnings, not a currency, and not a prize. Nothing in the Game is a wager, a bet, or a gamble, and no purchase is ever necessary to play or to earn Reward Points.

1. What the Rewards Program Is

Elden Earth is free to play. There is no way to deposit, purchase, stake or wager real money in the Game. By playing, you may earn Reward Points ("RP"), a loyalty balance that can occasionally be redeemed for a small monetary thank-you via PayPal, subject to the limits below.

RP is not money. It is not a currency, it cannot be traded, transferred or sold, and it has no value outside this program.

2. How Reward Points Are Earned

RP is earned through activity only. It is never awarded for chance outcomes.

  • Daily login streak
  • Daily quests (excluding the Earn Wheel)
  • Exploring and surveying new areas
  • Account tenure and other verifiable play activity

The Earn Wheel, lucky plot drops, and any other randomised mechanic pay out only in-game items (Elden Bucks and diamonds) which have no monetary value and cannot be redeemed. This separation is deliberate: no outcome determined by chance can produce anything of real-world value.

Earning is subject to per-source daily caps and a global daily cap, which are enforced automatically.

3. What Reward Points Are Worth

100 RP = $1.00 USD. Points are whole numbers; fractional points are not issued.

This rate may be changed prospectively under section 9. Changes never reduce points already earned.

4. Who Can Redeem

To redeem RP you must meet all of the following:

  • Be 18 years of age or older (you must be 13 or older to play at all)
  • Have a verified phone number linked to your account
  • Have a verified location in a region where redemption is permitted
  • Have an account at least 90 days old
  • Have no other redemption request currently pending

We verify these automatically. Your date of birth is recorded once and cannot be changed afterwards. Redemption is not available in all regions; eligibility is determined by your verified location at the time of the request.

5. Redemption Limits

Redemptions are deliberately small. They are a thank-you for playing, not an income stream.

  • Minimum redemption: 500 RP ($5.00)
  • Maximum single redemption: 500 RP ($5.00)
  • Per-player limit: $5.00 per calendar month
  • Global program limit: a monthly and a yearly ceiling on total redemptions across all players

There is also a short cooldown between redemptions. All limits are enforced automatically and cannot be waived.

6. The Realm Revenue Goal

Redemptions are funded by the Game's advertising revenue. Until that revenue covers the program's monthly redemption budget, redemptions are locked for everyone.

The Game shows a progress bar toward this goal on the rewards screen and in the Weekly Realm Treasury. The unlock is decided once per calendar month and, once granted, stays open for the rest of that month. When the goal is not met, redemptions remain locked and RP continues to accumulate normally.

You can track your RP balance at any time in your player profile, whether or not redemptions are currently open.

7. How Redemption Works

When you request a redemption, the corresponding RP is deducted immediately and held. Requests are reviewed manually and paid to the PayPal address you provide. The address must belong to you and must match your PayPal account.

  • Approval may take up to 7 business days.
  • If a request is rejected, the held RP is returned to your balance in full.
  • We are not responsible for payments sent to a PayPal address you entered incorrectly.

8. What This Program Is Not

  • Not gambling. No consideration is paid to participate, no wager is placed, and no outcome determined by chance produces real-world value.
  • Not employment or a source of income. No earnings are promised, and typical redemption amounts are a few dollars per month at most.
  • Not an investment. RP cannot appreciate, be transferred, or be inherited.
  • Not purchasable. There is no way to buy RP with real money or in-game items obtained with real money.

9. Changes, Suspension and Termination

We may change the earning rates, the redemption rate, the caps, or the regions where redemption is available at any time. Material changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

We may suspend or cancel the program, or refuse a redemption, where we reasonably believe there is fraud, cheating, abuse, or a legal or regulatory reason to do so. We may also suspend it if advertising revenue is insufficient to fund it.

If the program is terminated, RP balances are forfeited. Because RP is not money and was never purchased, no compensation is payable.

10. Taxes

Any amounts you receive may be taxable income. You are solely responsible for reporting and paying any taxes that apply to you.
