Rewards & Reward Points

Reward Points (RP) are earned by playing — never by spending. This page is the complete, no-fine-print explanation of how they work and how redemptions happen.

At a glance: 100 RP = $1.00 · earned through play only · redeemable to PayPal for verified players · never purchasable.

What is RP?

RP is Elden Earth's reward currency — think of it as a loyalty balance that reflects how much of the game you've actually played. It is a separate balance from the in-game currencies (EB, Realm Coins and diamonds have no cash value and can't be redeemed).

What never pays RP: anything chance-based. The Diamond Wheel pays EB and diamonds only — no game of chance can mint redeemable value. This is a deliberate design rule and it is not going away.

How redemptions work

  1. Verify your account. A quick phone verification links your player to a real person — this is required before any redemption.
  2. Reach the threshold. Minimum redemption is published in the in-game Rewards panel.
  3. Request a redemption. Choose an amount — 100 RP = $1.00 — and confirm your PayPal email.
  4. Review & payout. Requests are reviewed for account integrity and then paid via PayPal. Payouts open as the Realm's community revenue goal is met; the current status is shown in the Rewards panel.

The rules that keep it fair

  • RP is earned, never bought — there is no store for it
  • RP is non-transferable between players
  • Only one account per person may earn RP — multi-accounting forfeits balances
  • Automated play, bots or exploitation result in permanent loss of rewards eligibility
  • Rewards may be adjusted for regional availability and legal requirements

Heads up: rewards availability varies by region and law. Players under 18 or in regions where digital rewards aren't permitted can still earn RP — it just can't be redeemed. The full legal text is in the rewards terms.

Where to check your balance

Your RP balance is on your in-game profile and in the Rewards panel — and right here on the portal when you're signed in (top-right chip). Balances are stored server-side and can't be edited from a device.
